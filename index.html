Multi-line slideshow

Type one screen per line in the Text box. Each line shows for the time you set, then the next one. Lines wider than the panel scroll through.

"Send to panel" stores the slideshow on the panel as an animation, so it keeps running after you disconnect. Its timing depends on the time unit in Settings, so run "Test timing" there once. "Play from this device" times each screen exactly, but this page has to stay open.

Route 749 warp

About 47 s of neon rainbow stripes moving out from the center, then "Route" and "749" fly in, hold 0.3 s each, and fly past. Stripes clear behind each word (plus 6 columns each side). 720 frames.

Frame-limit test: count the white columns lit just before the animation restarts, then multiply by 20. A full bar means the panel holds all 720 frames.
